GOOD TRIPS START WITH A FEW GOOD CHOICES

Keep the plan light.
Keep the good parts close.

Use these as starting points, then make the trip your own.

01 · FIND YOUR PACE

Leave some room in the day

Choose one or two things you care about most. A little open time makes room for a slower morning, a local café, or a discovery you didn't plan.

02 · PLAN YOUR SPENDING

Give your budget a cushion

Think about transport, stays, meals, and experiences. Keep a small amount unplanned for the moments that make a trip memorable.

03 · GET TO KNOW THE PLACE

Look up local rhythms

Check how people get around, when places open, and whether a popular stop needs a reservation. Small details can make your day flow better.

04 · PACK FOR YOUR DAYS

Start with what you'll do

Build your packing list around the weather and your plans. Comfortable shoes, layers, and a light day bag are useful on many kinds of trips.

05 · STAY FLEXIBLE

Let the trip change a little

Keep important details together, then adjust when your energy, the weather, or a new recommendation points you somewhere else.
