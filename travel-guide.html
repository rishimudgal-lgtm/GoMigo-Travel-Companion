A FEW THOUGHTFUL REMINDERS

Travel well, your way.

Useful starting points for a smoother, more personal trip.

01 · BEFORE YOU GO

Set a comfortable pace

Plan one or two key experiences each day and leave space for rest, delays, and unexpected finds.

02 · YOUR BUDGET

Keep a little room

Split your budget across travel, stay, food, and activities. Hold some back for the experience you discover along the way.

03 · LOCAL DETAILS

Learn the small things

Check local transport, opening days, cultural customs, and whether you need cash or advance reservations.

04 · PACKING

Pack for your itinerary

Think about the weather and the activities you chose. Comfortable shoes and a light day bag make many plans easier.

05 · STAY FLEXIBLE

Let the trip breathe

Keep important bookings together, but be willing to swap a stop when the weather or your energy changes.
